⛳ How to Play

Objective

This is a 5-hole bar crawl played by golf rules: lowest total score wins! The twist is that the lowest score belongs to whoever drank the most.

Scoring Scale (Per Hole)

Drinks Golf Term Score
0 drinks Bogey +1
1 drink Par E (0)
2 drinks Birdie -1
3 drinks Eagle -2
4 drinks Albatross -3
5+ drinks (Uncapped) -4, -5, etc.

What Counts as a Drink?

  • Any beer, cocktail, or glass of wine.
  • A shot.
  • Buying Colin a drink: Lowers the score of the buyer only (does not credit Colin's card). Feel free to buy him drinks to lower your score!

Bonuses (−1 stroke each)

Earn once per hole:

  • Recruited a stranger: Convinced a stranger to join the crawl.
  • Won a bar game: Win any game at the bar.
  • Gave a toast: Give a toast to the group.
  • Signature challenge: Complete the specific challenge for that hole.

Penalties (+1 stroke each)

Filed by other players (no self-penalizing!):

  • Spilled a drink.
  • Didn't finish a drink (shots included).
  • Left a bar before the group.
  • Passed on a round everyone else joined.

Disputes & False Filings

If you disagree with a penalty filed against you, you can dispute it. If 2 other players agree with your dispute, the penalty is voided. However, the player who filed the false penalty receives an automatic +1 False Filing penalty on that hole!
