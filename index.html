About

I'm a neuroscientist and engineer working at the intersection of electrophysiology, biomedical engineering, and pharmacology. I'm drawn to questions where the tools to answer them don't exist yet.

At Emory, co-mentored by Dr. Nicholas Au Yong and Dr. Alan Emanuel, my research targets spinal shock: the immediate collapse of motor and reflex function after spinal cord injury, a phenomenon that has never been observed at single-neuron resolution. Using a high-density intraspinal recording platform I helped develop, I track living spinal circuits through the moment of injury — and test whether targeted pharmacology can bring them back.

Research

  1. 2024 —

    Emory University

    Graduate Researcher · Au Yong & Emanuel Labs

    Developing a Neuropixels-based intraspinal recording platform and a mouse model of spinal shock — recording hundreds of single units across the lumbar cord during acute injury. Mapping the neural targets of epidural stimulation and building deep-learning pipelines for behavioral analysis of touch.

  2. 2022 — 24

    Georgia Institute of Technology

    Research Technician · Fong Lab

    Designed and open-sourced a complete platform for synchronized cortical electrophysiology and visual stimulation. Used it to discover ON-pathway-specific cortical potentiation — a finding with implications for treating amblyopia and myopia, now a pending utility patent.

  3. 2021

    University of Florida

    SNIP Fellow · Dale Lab

    Closed-loop epidural stimulation to restore diaphragm motor output after cervical spinal cord injury — recording and stimulating in the same breath.
